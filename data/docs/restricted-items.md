# Restricted Items Policy

Mock policy for the Portside demo. Not real carrier or customs rules.

## Lithium batteries: general rules

Portside classifies every shipment containing a lithium battery by how the battery is packed. Merchants must set the battery flag on the item and declare the battery type, watt-hour rating and UN number before a label is created. Shipments with undeclared batteries are held at the origin hub.

- UN3481: lithium-ion cells or batteries packed with or contained in equipment. Accepted, subject to the limits below.
- UN3091: lithium metal batteries packed with or contained in equipment. Accepted on PortExpress only.
- UN3480: standalone lithium-ion batteries, including power banks. Not accepted on any Portside service.
- UN3090: standalone lithium metal batteries. Not accepted on any Portside service.

## Lithium batteries: limits per package

A package under UN3481 may contain up to 2 batteries, each rated at 100 Wh or less. Batteries must be protected against short circuit, and the device must be switched off and secured against accidental activation. Each package must carry a lithium battery mark. Batteries that are damaged, recalled or swollen are not accepted.

## Lithium batteries: carrier acceptance

- PortExpress accepts UN3481 and UN3091 to all destinations.
- SilkLine Air accepts UN3481 only, and requires the battery to be installed in the device.
- BlueWave Post does not accept any lithium battery shipment.

## Lithium batteries to Germany and the EU

Shipments containing batteries to Germany or any other EU destination must use PortExpress or SilkLine Air. The merchant must hold a registered battery producer number for each EU country of sale. For Germany, this is the LUCID-style packaging and battery registration number, and it must be supplied before the first shipment.

Under DDP terms, Portside pays import duties and VAT on the merchant's behalf, but DDP does not change battery acceptance rules. A DDP shipment to Germany containing a UN3481 device is accepted when the carrier, watt-hour and registration requirements are met. Missing registration numbers cause a customs hold at the German gateway, and any storage fees are charged to the merchant.

## Liquids

Liquids up to 100 ml per container and 500 ml per package are accepted if sealed inside a leak-proof inner bag. Flammable liquids, including perfume and nail polish, are accepted on PortExpress only, and only up to 60 ml per container.

## Magnets

Products with strong magnets must be declared. Air services require magnetic field strength below 0.00525 gauss at 2.1 metres from the package. Shipments above that limit may move only on BlueWave Post surface service.

## Prohibited items

The following are not accepted on any Portside service: compressed gas, aerosols above 100 ml, explosives and fireworks, weapons and replica weapons, cannabis products, prescription medicine, and live animals.
