# Carrier Service Levels

Mock policy for the Portside demo. Not real carrier or customs rules.

## PortExpress

Express air service from Shenzhen and Yiwu. Delivers in 4 to 7 business days to the EU, UK, US and Canada, and in 5 to 8 business days to Australia and New Zealand. Includes door-to-door tracking and insurance up to USD 200.

## SilkLine Air

Economy air service. Delivers in 7 to 12 business days to the EU, UK, US and Canada, and in 9 to 14 business days to Australia and New Zealand. Includes tracking and insurance up to USD 100.

## BlueWave Post

Postal and surface service for low-value parcels. Delivers in 15 to 30 business days. Tracking stops at the destination country on orders under USD 15. Includes insurance up to USD 25.

## How delivery time is measured

Delivery times start when the carrier scans the parcel at the origin hub and exclude customs holds, public holidays and weather events. An estimated delivery date is a target and not a guarantee, unless a late delivery credit applies.

## Late delivery credits

If a PortExpress shipment arrives more than 3 business days after the service level, the merchant can claim a credit of 20% of the shipping fee. If a SilkLine Air shipment arrives more than 5 business days late, the credit is 10%. BlueWave Post has no late credit. Claims must be filed within 30 days of delivery.

## Lost parcels

A parcel with no tracking update for 10 business days on PortExpress or SilkLine Air, or 20 business days on BlueWave Post, can be opened as a lost parcel investigation. Investigations take up to 15 business days. If the parcel is declared lost, Portside refunds the shipping fee and pays the declared value up to the insurance limit.
