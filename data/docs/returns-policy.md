# Returns and Claims Policy

Mock policy for the Portside demo. Not real carrier or customs rules.

## Customer returns

Merchants choose between two return options. Local return uses a Portside consolidation address in Germany, the UK or the US, and costs less. Return to origin sends the parcel back to the Shenzhen or Yiwu hub, and takes 15 to 30 business days. Return shipping is charged to the merchant.

## Refused and undeliverable parcels

A parcel refused by the customer, or undeliverable after 3 delivery attempts, is held for 7 days and then returned to origin. The merchant pays return shipping and any destination fees. Duties paid on DDP shipments are generally not recoverable.

## Damaged parcels

Merchants must file a damage claim within 14 days of delivery and include photos of the item, outer box and shipping label. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed. Approved claims pay the declared value up to the carrier's insurance limit. Claims are reviewed within 10 business days.

## Items that cannot be returned

Parcels containing lithium batteries may be returned only if the battery is undamaged, and only on PortExpress. Damaged batteries cannot be shipped and must be disposed of locally by the customer, following local rules. Opened cosmetics and perishable goods are not returned.

## Disposal and abandonment

If a merchant doesn't respond within 30 days of a return arriving at the hub, Portside may dispose of the goods. Storage is free for the first 14 days, then USD 1 per parcel per day.
